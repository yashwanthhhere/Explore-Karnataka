About Karnataka

Karnataka is a vibrant state located in southwestern India. It is celebrated for its historic empires, stunning Western Ghats mountains, serene beaches, and dynamic technological capital, Bengaluru.

Tourist & Historical Places

Hampi

UNESCO World Heritage site with ancient ruins and temples of the Vijayanagara Empire.

Mysore Palace

Magnificent royal palace known for its Indo-Saracenic design and festive light displays.

Badami Caves

Historic 6th-century rock-cut cave temples crafted from golden sandstone cliffs.

Waterfalls & Hill Stations

Jog Falls

A breathtaking cascade on the Sharavathi River and one of India's tallest waterfalls.

Coorg

Scenic hill station known for lush coffee plantations, misty hills, and fresh spices.

Chikmagalur

Famous for serene trekking paths and being the coffee cradle of Karnataka.

Famous Foods

Bisi Bele Bath

Traditional hot and spicy rice dish cooked with lentils, vegetables, and aromatic spices.

Mysore Pak

A royal melt-in-mouth traditional sweet made with pure ghee, sugar, and chickpea flour.

Neer Dosa

Soft, light rice crepes from coastal Karnataka, served with coconut chutney and sambar.

Travel Information

How to Reach

By Air: Bengaluru International Airport and Mangaluru Airport connect major cities.

By Train: Extensive railway network connecting Bengaluru, Mysuru, and Hubballi.

By Road: Excellent national highways and KSRTC state buses provide easy transport.

Best Time to Visit

October to March offers cool, pleasant weather for sightseeing across the state.
